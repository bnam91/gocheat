GOSHOT

Capture your Windows screen and finish annotating, blurring, and OCR right where you shot it.

Download

Windows 10 or later · Free

Capture · polish · share in one flow

Instead of opening and closing a separate capture tool, you polish right where you shot it.

The moment you capture the screen, GOSHOT pops up an editing overlay alongside it, so you lay arrows, numbers, text, and mosaics right on top of it without moving to another app. A CleanShot X-style flow, on Windows.

The text on your screen becomes real text through offline OCR (no internet needed, Korean and English), and the result is dressed up with a background and shadow into an image ready to use as is — save, copy, or share it.

Capture, editing, and OCR all happen on your own PC — the screen image is never sent to a server (the only exception is when you create a share link yourself). You log in with your 소문의섬 account, and every feature is currently free.

Capture a region or the full screen, draw and cover right on top of it, pull text out of the pixels, dress it up with a background, then save, copy, or share. It works on the primary monitor.

Draw right where you shot it

Mark things up with arrows, rectangles, text, numbers (1, 2, 3…), and highlights, and cover what needs hiding with blur, mosaic, or spotlight. Crop and undo (Ctrl+Z) work too.

Turn on-screen text into text (OCR)

It reads the text in the selected region and copies it to your clipboard. Korean and English are processed without internet, on your own PC, without sending the image to a server.

Dress it up with a background

Gradient or solid backgrounds, padding, shadow, and rounded corners, plus 1:1, 4:5, and 16:9 ratios. Polish it into a shape you can post straight to a presentation or social media.

Keep it floating while you work

Pin a capture as a small always-on-top window so you can keep a reference on screen while doing other work. You can float several at once.

Right after the shot, so you never stop

A preview card appears in the corner right after capture, so you can edit, copy, save, pin, or share on the spot. The shortcuts are Ctrl+Shift+2 for a region and Ctrl+Shift+3 for the full screen (changeable).

How to use

Capture, polish, export.

  1. Drag a region with the shortcut (Ctrl+Shift+2), or capture the full screen (Ctrl+Shift+3).
  2. In the editing overlay that pops up alongside, polish it with annotations, mosaic, OCR, and background styling.
  3. Export by saving a PNG, copying to the clipboard, pinning, or making a share link — done.

Running it requires logging in with your 소문의섬 account (every feature is currently free). If you don't have one, sign up first.
