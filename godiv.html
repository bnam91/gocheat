GODIV Product Image Downloader

Save every image on a product detail page — in order — with a single click.

See how it works

Chrome extension · coming soon to the Web Store.

It doesn't photograph the screen — it fetches the originals

Not a screenshot — the original image files exactly as they were uploaded to the detail page.

GODIV does not capture the detail page as a screen image. It downloads the original image files that are actually on the page. So the quality never degrades, and it keeps the same sharpness the images had when they were first uploaded.

Screen-capture tools like GoFullPage photograph what's visible on screen, so text and fine detail get smeared — but GODIV pulls the originals, so you get the same quality you can reuse in your own product listings.

Made for online store operators who list products and the designers who work on detail pages. It will integrate with the detail-page editing tool goditor (GODITOR) in the future.

It saves the body images on the product detail page you're currently viewing in your browser to your downloads folder, top to bottom, in the same order. Supports Naver Smart Store / Brand Store, Coupang, and Wadiz.

Gets everything, to the end

It automatically expands collapsed detail sections and waits for late-loading images before collecting them. It always shows how many it found and how many it saved (e.g. "Done: 33/33 images"). If some fail, it doesn't pretend everything worked — it shows you exactly why.

In order, neatly foldered

It numbers images 01, 02, 03… in the order they appear on the detail page and saves them to the Downloads › godiv › product name folder.

Stays inside your browser

It only reads pages you've already opened yourself. It doesn't re-scrape pages through a separate server, and it doesn't send which page you downloaded what from to any server.

How to use

Three clicks and you're done.

  1. Open a product detail page on a supported site.
  2. Log in to the GODIV panel at the top right with your GODITOR account.
  3. Click "Download images on this page" — done.

You use it by logging in with a goditor (GODITOR) account. If you don't have one, sign up first.

Privacy Policy

Full privacy policy

1. Information we collect and use

  • Email address: used for account login and to verify your subscription.
  • Password: sent to our server over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) at login for verification only; it is not stored in the extension.
  • Session token: stored only in the browser's extension storage to keep you logged in.
  • Usage records (added 2026-08-25): each time you run a download, we record which supported site it ran on (Naver / Coupang / Wadiz), how many images were found and saved, whether it succeeded, and the time. These records are stored against your account (email) together with your plan type, because they are counted per account. Purpose: preventing account abuse, detecting when a supported site breaks the extension, and deciding what to improve.

2. Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect browsing history, page addresses (URLs), page titles, product names, page contents, search terms, location data, or financial information.
  • The images themselves never leave your device — downloading happens entirely inside your browser. We record that a download happened and how many images it saved, never the images and never the address of the page.
  • Nothing is recorded while you merely browse. A record is created only at the moment you press the download button.
  • One exception, and it is not automatic: if you use the "report an error to the developer" link, your mail app opens with the address of the page you were on included in the message body, so we can reproduce the problem. You send that mail yourself and can read or delete that line before sending. Nothing is transmitted unless you send it.

3. Retention and destruction

  • The session token is deleted immediately when you log out.
  • Individual usage records are automatically deleted after at most 180 days. Aggregate counters are kept while the account exists and are destroyed when the account is deleted. To be exact, those counters are: total downloads, total images, total attempts and failures, first and most recent use time, per-site download counts, the most recently used site, and the most recent plan type. None of them contain a page address, page title, or product name.
  • Retention and destruction of account information follow the goditor (GODITOR) service terms and privacy policy.

4. Provision to third parties

  • We do not sell or provide collected information to any third party.
