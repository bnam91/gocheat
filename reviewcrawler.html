It gathers Naver and Coupang product reviews — ratings, text, date written, and photos — and saves them as Excel and JSON. For Naver, it also collects product Q&A.

Naver and Coupang in one tool

It collects product reviews from Naver Smart Store / Brand Store and Coupang the same way. For Naver, it can also include product Q&A — questions and answers together.

Ratings, text, date, and photos

It gathers each review's star rating, author display name, date written, body text, review type (regular / one-month use / repurchase), and attached photos.

As Excel and JSON, on your PC

It saves the results as Excel (.xlsx) and JSON at the same time. In the Excel file, review photos are embedded as images inside the cells. Every file stays in the save folder on your own computer.

Paste a URL, or search

Paste a product URL and it goes straight to work; enter a search term and it picks a product from the search results to collect. You can also choose how many pages to collect (5 / 15 / 50 / max).

A real browser, without overloading

It opens a real Chrome window and collects exactly what a person sees on screen, and when requests pile up it waits a moment on its own, then resumes — so it doesn't overload the target site.

How to use

Paste, collect, and it's in Excel.

  1. Paste a Naver or Coupang product URL, or find a product by search term.
  2. Choose how many pages to collect and start — it gathers the reviews in order in a Chrome window.
  3. When it finishes, Excel (.xlsx) and JSON files are created in the save folder — photos included.

For Naver, once you log in with your own account the first time, it continues automatically after that. Please use the collected data in compliance with the target platform's terms of service and applicable laws.

Full privacy policy

1. Information we collect and use (user)

  • Access IP address for usage registration (device verification): used for license verification and stored on our server.
  • (Optional) Diagnostic logs: only when you turn on "Send diagnostic logs", information such as the app version, run environment, the product URL you entered, and the number of items collected is sent for the purpose of fixing errors. Review text, author information, and images are not included.

2. Handling of the collected review data

  • Reviews collected from the target product page (ratings, author display name, body text, photos, etc.) are saved entirely as files on your own PC and are not sent to our server.
  • The collected data may include third-party information such as the review author's public display name, so you must use it in compliance with the target platform's terms of service and applicable laws.

3. Retention and destruction

  • You manage the storage and deletion of the collected result files yourself.
  • Retention and destruction of account information, such as the IP used for license verification, follow the 소문의섬 service policy.

  • We do not sell or provide the user information we hold to any third party.
